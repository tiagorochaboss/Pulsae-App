# Pulsae v5 - what changed

Customer app
- Saved addresses: new pencil icon on every address to edit label, street, notes and default.
- Discover > professional page: photos and videos strip under the name (only shown if the professional added any). Tap to open full screen; videos play when expo-video is installed.
- Community: much more colour. Daily motivational banner, colour-coded topics with emoji, illustrated post and event cards, calendar badge on events.
- Language button (EN / عربي) at the top of every main screen, next to the bell.
- The "Profile" tab is now "Settings", with the same cog icon as the professional app.
- If a professional changes their session price, customers now see and pay the new price.

Professional app
- Tab "Profile" renamed "Professional" with an award icon (name and icon are placeholders until you choose).
- Price per session moves up and down in steps of SAR 25 (SAR 100 to SAR 1,000).
- Moved from Settings to Professional: Availability, Service area, Saved places, Clients, Reviews, Earnings, Bank account, Credentials and documents.
- Settings now only holds account management: personal details, login and security, notifications, language, legal, help, contact us.
- New "Photos and videos" screen: add from library or camera, up to 12 items, remove any.
- More colour: coral header with rating stats on Professional; coral earnings card with Confirmed / Waiting / Completed tiles on Bookings; same lively Community.

Install after unzipping (one time):
  npx expo install expo-video
(expo-secure-store, expo-local-authentication and expo-image-picker from v4 are still needed.)
